# VPN troubleshooting

Symptoms:
- Cannot connect to corporate VPN.
- VPN connects and immediately disconnects.

Checks:
1. Confirm internet access.
2. Confirm the device date/time is correct.
3. Restart the VPN client.
4. Capture the exact error message.
5. If the client reports a certificate or identity error, escalate to the security/service desk team.

Do not request or store a user's VPN password.
